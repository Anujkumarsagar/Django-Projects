Building the Future of Work

We're on a mission to empower teams worldwide with tools that make collaboration seamless, efficient, and enjoyable.

Our Story

CloudFlow was born in 2019 from a simple observation: teams were struggling with disconnected tools, endless meetings, and lost productivity. Our founders, having experienced these challenges firsthand at leading tech companies, knew there had to be a better way.

We started with a vision to create a platform that brings everything together—communication, project management, automation, and analytics—in one beautiful, intuitive interface. What began as a small team working from a garage has grown into a global company serving millions of users across 150+ countries.

Today, we're proud to be the trusted partner for companies ranging from ambitious startups to Fortune 500 enterprises. But our mission remains the same: make work better for everyone.
